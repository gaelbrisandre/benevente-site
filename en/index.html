FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

Everything you need to know before running your first event with BénéVente.

Do volunteers need an account?

No. The organizer shares the event QR code and volunteers open the checkout directly on their smartphones.

Do I need to install an app?

No. BénéVente works directly in a browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Do I need special equipment?

No. An internet-connected smartphone is enough.

Does BénéVente process my customers’ payments?

No. BénéVente records and tracks sales. Your organization collects customer payments using its own payment methods.

Can several volunteers sell at once?

Yes. Multiple volunteers can use the event checkout simultaneously from their own phones.

Can I track inventory?

Yes. Sales are centralized and product stock levels update as sales are recorded.

What happens after 30 sales?

Les 30 premières ventes de chaque événement sont gratuites. Pour continuer à enregistrer des ventes, vous pouvez débloquer l'événement avec Premium Événement ou utiliser Premium Annuel.

What's the difference between Event and Annual Premium?

Event Premium unlocks unlimited sales for one event with no subscription. Annual Premium gives you unlimited sales across your events for one year.
